No new theory, just a challenge that uses the HTML of this part: real buttons, not ARIA, buttons that say they are pressed, and icons only for the eye.

Some visitors feel sick when things move on a screen. The settings page should let them ask for less movement. The animations come later in this part. Today, the buttons.

## Mission: Less motion

**What you will see**: under the theme part, a part with a heading like "Motion", a sentence about it, and two buttons, `✦ Full` and `◌ Less`. Full is pressed, and looks pressed, like the System button.

- After the theme part, add a new part with its own heading.
- In it, add a box with the id `motion-buttons`.
- The box holds two real buttons that send no form: `motion-full`, which says `Full`, and `motion-less`, which says `Less`.
- Full says it's pressed, and Less says it isn't, the way a screen reader understands.
- Each button has an icon before its word, which a screen reader skips. You choose the icons.
- The pressed button looks different from the other one.
- Both are easy to read, with a contrast of at least 4.5.

The pressed look of the theme buttons can count for these buttons too.
