## Mission: Tools & Spare Parts

**What you will see**: the new page looks like the greenhouse and airlock pages. It has the site's header and menu, the heading **Tools & Spare Parts** with its parts, a box of facts after them, and the footer.

Here is what Tomas wrote:

```text
Tools & Spare Parts
- a broken pump can't wait for the next supply ship, so the station keeps its own tools and spare parts on board
- where they are: the cargo bay, on the lower deck; Tomas Okafor, the engineer, keeps a list of every part
- nothing falls on the station, it floats: every tool is tied to a belt, and every box of parts closes with a lid
- facts: 1,200 spare parts; the heaviest tool weighs 12 kg; the longest cable is 30 m
```

- Add a new page, `wiki-tools.html`, built like the other wiki pages, from the header and its named menu to the footer.
- Its tab and its biggest heading say `Tools & Spare Parts`.
- Its article tells the topic in at least two parts, each with its own heading.
- Its fact box, with the class `fact-box`, has a heading.
- The fact box has at least two numbers with their units, each one a `measure` that never breaks, with the HTML alone.
- It has its own description, which names the tools.
- It's made for the phone's width, like the other pages.
- On the wiki's front page, the Tools & Spare Parts entry now goes to the new page.

The `&` in the title is special in HTML, in the `<title>` too.
