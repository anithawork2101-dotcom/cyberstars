## Mission: Starship cards

**What you will see**: on a wide window, the cards stand in two columns, with a little space between them. Each card has its own background, round corners and space inside it. The status, like `Docked`, sits in a small colored badge. On a phone, the cards stand one under the other, each as wide as the list.

Soon, the tracker fills its list with a card for each starship, made from your template. Give those cards a look before they arrive, so the page is ready for them.

- Each starship card (`.ship`) is at least 60px tall.
- It has at least 12px of space inside it at the top and on the left.
- It has round corners (at least 8px), and a background that stands out from the page's.
- The status stands out from the name with its own background.
- The name, the class and the status are each easy to read on what is behind them, with a contrast of at least 4.5, in the light colors and in the dark ones.
- On a wide window, the list stands in two columns, at least 8px apart, with the cards' tops lined up in a row.
- On a phone, it's one column, with the cards at least 8px apart, each one as wide as the list.
- Nothing comes out sideways.
- The skeleton cards stand the same way, because they are items of the same list.

The cards are items of the list `ships`, so the list is the grid.
